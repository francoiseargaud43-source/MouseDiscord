📏 Mouse Size
Based on your measurements, look for mice within these ranges:

Optimal Mouse Length: 126.7mm - 131.7mm (*Measured along the full length, from the rear base to the front tip)*

Optimal Mouse Width: 57.2mm - 61.2mm (*Measured with the mouse upside down, at the sensor position)*

Rear Design
Narrow Rear: Since your thenar muscles are average sized, choose a mouse with a narrow, pill-shaped rear.

Hump Placement
Mid-to-Back: A hump placed in the middle or toward the back is best suited for different claw grip variations. The hump is meant to support your palmar transverse arch, which is determined by the grip variation you use.

Claw Grip Variants
Relaxed Claw Grip: Best paired with a prominent back hump
Aggressive Claw Grip: Best paired with a prominent middle hump
Pincer Claw Grip (pinching the mouse, pinky placed further back on the side): Best paired with a back hump and less curvature on the sides

Button Height
Low: Claw grippers benefit from a lower button height to maximize comfort. High clicks keep your extensor tendons under constant tension to pull back your fingers. A lower profile allows them to remain relaxed.

Mouse Design
While low-profile ergonomic shapes can work for a claw grip, symmetrical mice are generally superior, offering more benefits for all variations of the claw grip style.
