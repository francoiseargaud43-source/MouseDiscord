Rear Design
Narrow Rear: Since your thenar muscles are on the smaller side, choose a mouse with a narrow, pill-shaped rear.

Hump Placement
Mid-to-Front: Since you utilize a palm grip, a hump positioned toward the center or front is ideal. This shape follows the natural arch of your hand, providing stability without limiting your wrist's range of motion.

Button Height
Elevated: For your flat laying fingers, a higher button height is recommended. This prevents your extensor tendons from being under constant stretch when gripping the mouse tightly.

Mouse Design
